Honest answers

Things parents actually ask.

Is this another app that's going to make me feel guilty?

No red badges. No "you missed yesterday" shame spiral. Skip a day, skip a week — your sprout just naps and waits for you. The whole point is to lower the stakes, not raise them.

How are the plans and their steps chosen?

Each plan breaks one skill into rungs small enough that the next is genuinely within reach — the same task-analysis approach used in early-years teaching and occupational therapy. Several plans follow a named method end to end: Montessori's practical life exercises, Gottman's emotion coaching, Brazelton's child-led readiness. The tone and the scripts come from the same literature we summarise on the reading side. Warm, simple, and doable on a chaotic Tuesday morning. No flashcard nonsense.

What's the difference between a guide and a book summary?

A guide is ours. We write it about one specific problem you're having tonight — the toys on the floor at 8pm, what to do after you lose it — and it takes about five minutes. A book summary is somebody else's: the actual argument of a real parenting book, written out in about twelve minutes so you get the thinking rather than a listicle of its chapter titles.

Both end the same way — by handing you the plan that turns the idea into something your child practises.

Are the book summaries the actual books?

No, and they're not meant to replace them. We write our own summaries, in our own prose, of the ideas in each book. We're not affiliated with the authors and they haven't endorsed us. If a summary lands and you want the whole argument, go and buy the book — that's the point of reading it with us first.

Does it work with multiple kids?

Yes. Each child gets their own profile, their own sprout and their own plans — a three-year-old learning to dress themselves and a five-year-old learning to write their name run side by side. Switch between them with a tap. The guides and book summaries are shared, because they're for you, not them.

Is it just an iOS app?

We're on iOS and Android — same features, same prices. Web version is coming.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No emails to send, no "are you sure" loops. Where you cancel depends on where you subscribed — see the next question. The free tier stays free for as long as you want it.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Wherever you bought it is where you manage it. That's the rule the app stores enforce, and it means we can't change or cancel a store subscription on your behalf from our side.

  • Subscribed inside the iPhone or iPad app? Apple handles the billing. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, or go straight to your App Store subscriptions (Apple's step-by-step guide).
  • Subscribed inside the Android app? Google Play handles the billing. Open Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or go straight to your Play Store subscriptions (Google's step-by-step guide).
  • Subscribed here on the web? We bill through Stripe. Open your billing portal to switch plans, update your card, or cancel — it takes effect immediately and you keep access through the period you've already paid for.

Not sure which one you used? Start at the billing portal — sign in with the same email or Google account you subscribed with, and it will either open your Stripe billing page or tell you that Apple or Google is handling it instead.
