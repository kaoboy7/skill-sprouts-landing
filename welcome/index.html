The Skill Sprouts method

Calmer parenting is just
tiny reps, repeated.

The hard parenting moments rarely turn on one big talk — they shift through what you do again and again. Behavior science is clear: small, consistent actions rewire how you respond faster than big resolutions that fizzle by Thursday. And your kids learn the pattern by watching yours.

1
Pick one tiny parenting habit
Two minutes, anchored to a moment you already have — the buckle-up, the bedtime tuck-in, the dinner table.
2

A gentle reminder, a one-tap check-in. No streak-guilt, no shame spirals when a hard day happens.
3
Watch it change the room
Fewer meltdowns, smoother mornings — and a sprout that grows as your consistency does. Small wins, made visible.
